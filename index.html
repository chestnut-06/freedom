Freedom

Cobalt blue skies, a giant red bridge, strong winds blowing through my hair, people passing me by…

The freedom and sense of release in that wide-open space… The Golden Gate Bridge is the place I imagine when my soul longs to be free.

After coming home from physically and mentally exhausting days, I worked on this diamond painting. A week-long trip, a fleeting moment, and about 24,300 tiny gems placed one by one, each holding a memory.

This bridge gave me a moment when I felt that it was okay to simply be, without doing anything or thinking about anything. Freedom. To me, that is the soul of this bridge.

To someone else, it may have been just an ordinary bridge. To me, it held a feeling of freedom that nothing else could quite recreate. I hope this piece shares the same feeling with others.
